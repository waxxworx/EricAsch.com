Introduction to Mindfulness Meditation

A Four-Week Introductory Course

"Meditation is not evasion; it is a serene encounter with reality."

— Thich Nhất Hạnh

A four-week journey into the foundations of mindfulness practice — learning to rest in awareness, work skillfully with emotions, open the heart, and carry practice into everyday life.

Why Mindfulness?

Research consistently shows that a regular mindfulness practice can reduce stress and anxiety, improve emotional regulation, deepen self-awareness, strengthen focus and clarity, and support greater calm in everyday life — not by fixing what's broken, but by helping you meet what's already here.
